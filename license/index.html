Sent

Your key is on its way to your email. It usually arrives within a minute. Check the spam folder if it does not.

The email carries the download link and the key. Open the program, click Paste license key, and paste the block from the email.

What happens next

  1. The email arrives with a download link and your license key.
  2. Put the program in a folder of its own and open it.
  3. It asks for a license. Click Paste license key and paste the block from the email.

The key locks to the first computer that opens it. If you need it on a second machine, ask and you will get a second key — copying the license across will not work.

The program checks in with this site about once a month. It does not need the internet while you work; it needs it once in the month. Somewhere with no signal at all for weeks on end is worth mentioning when you ask, and the key can be issued to suit.
